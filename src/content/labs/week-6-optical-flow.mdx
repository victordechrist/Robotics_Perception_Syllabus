# Lab 6: Optical Flow & Tracking

Track corners through a video with pyramidal Lucas-Kanade, gate tracks by the structure-tensor eigenvalues, and validate with forward-backward checks.

<InfoCallout title="Prerequisites">
Review Lecture 6 (Optical Flow & Motion Tracking) — the concepts below build directly on it.
</InfoCallout>

## Warm-Up

Compute `A^T A` eigenvalues for windows on a flat region, an edge, and a corner. Which can constrain two components of flow?

## Core

Track Shi-Tomasi corners with `calcOpticalFlowPyrLK` over 100 frames. Keep only tracks passing the minimum-eigenvalue gate AND the forward-backward check (< 1 px). Draw the surviving trails.

## Boss Level

Compare sparse LK to dense Farneback on the same clip. Where does each fail — large motion, motion blur, textureless regions?

<Warning title="Watch Out">
Gating on the maximum eigenvalue — that accepts edges, where only perpendicular motion is observable.
</Warning>
