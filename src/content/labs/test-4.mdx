---

## How the test works

The test lab has **two parts** in the tabs above:

- **Workspace** — a consensus reasoning exercise with **eight scenes** (double the usual three). Each scene presents a concept from the labs above; build your reasoning in the tree and commit thoughts as you would in any lab. The tree is scored against a gold tree covering all four topics.
- **Quiz** — **twelve auto-graded multiple-choice questions** covering the same four labs. Answer all of them; you get instant feedback with an explanation after each answer. Your best score is saved, so you can retake it.

## What is being assessed

- **Knowledge** — do you know the mechanisms and definitions from the labs above?
- **Experience** — can you apply them in the reasoning scenes, connecting concepts across labs?
- **Insight** — can you explain *why* each design choice matters?

This is the last checkpoint before the capstone showcase.
