# Lab 9: Noise Models & Covariance Propagation

Model sensor noise as correlated Gaussians, propagate covariance through a nonlinear motion model, and read the result as a confidence ellipse.

<InfoCallout title="Prerequisites">
Review Lecture 9 (Probability & Sensor Noise Models) — the concepts below build directly on it.
</InfoCallout>

## Warm-Up

Sample 10,000 draws from a correlated Gaussian via `chol(Σ)` and confirm the empirical covariance reproduces the off-diagonals.

## Core

Propagate a unicycle pose covariance over 50 steps with `Σ′ = FΣFᵀ + Q`. Plot the 95% ellipse every 10 steps and watch it grow and rotate.

## Boss Level

Compare first-order propagation against a Monte Carlo cloud through a hard turn. When does linearization stop covering 95%?

<Warning title="Watch Out">
Scaling each axis by its stddev instead of Cholesky — that produces axis-aligned noise and erases the correlation structure.
</Warning>
