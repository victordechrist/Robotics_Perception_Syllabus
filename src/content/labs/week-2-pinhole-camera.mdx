# Lab 2: Pinhole Camera & Lens Distortion

Implement the full projection chain — world frame to pixel — and see exactly where depth is lost, and what lens distortion does to straight lines.

<InfoCallout title="Prerequisites">
Review Lecture 2 (Pinhole Cameras & Projective Geometry) — the concepts below build directly on it.
</InfoCallout>

## Warm-Up

Project eight cube corners 4 m in front of a camera (`fx = fy = 500`, `cx = cy = 320`). Verify the two corners on the optical axis map to the principal point.

## Core

Implement `project(P_world, R, t, K, dist)`: transform to camera frame, perspective-divide, apply Brown-Conrady distortion in normalized coordinates, multiply by `K`. Verify on synthetic points with known answers.

## Boss Level

Add radial distortion (`k1 = -0.25`) to a synthetic grid and visualize how straight lines bow. Then implement `undistortPoints` numerically and show it straightens them back.

<Warning title="Watch Out">
Distorting in pixel space instead of normalized coordinates — the coefficients were fit for a scale-free input.
</Warning>
