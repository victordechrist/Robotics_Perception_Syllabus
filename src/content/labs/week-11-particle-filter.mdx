# Lab 11: Monte Carlo Localization

Localize a robot with a particle filter: propagate hypotheses, weight by likelihood, and resample before degeneracy.

<InfoCallout title="Prerequisites">
Review Lecture 11 (Particle Filtering & Monte Carlo Localization) — the concepts below build directly on it.
</InfoCallout>

## Warm-Up

Scatter 1,000 particles uniformly over a map and plot the cloud. Then re-weight by a single range measurement and watch it concentrate.

## Core

Implement MCL: per-particle motion + noise, likelihood weighting, normalization, resampling gated by `N_eff = 1/Σw²`. Show global localization converging.

## Boss Level

Kidnap the robot mid-run (teleport it without telling the filter). Add random-particle injection and measure steps to recovery.

<Warning title="Watch Out">
Never resampling — within a few steps one particle holds all weight and the other 999 are wasted.
</Warning>

Work through the tiers above, then apply the same pipeline in this lab's **Exercise** tab (ordering the steps) and argue your design in the **Workspace** reasoning tree. Save your code and figures — they feed your capstone.
